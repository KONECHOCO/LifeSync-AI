LifeSync AI is your private, on-device daily companion — it quietly gathers the moments of your day and turns them into a clear, readable story every evening.

WHAT LIFESYNC AI DOES
• Battery-friendly location log — uses significant-location-change and visit detection to note where your day took you, using less than 0.8% battery.
• Screen time for messaging — see how many minutes you spent on calls and messaging apps, fully within Apple's DeviceActivity privacy rules.
• One-tap voice journaling — record a quick thought and let LifeSync AI transcribe and fold it into your daily backup.
• Automatic AI daily summary — every evening at 11:00 PM, LifeSync AI writes a short narrative of your day from your activity, location, and voice notes.
• Encrypted backup — your daily logs are stored locally and, optionally, in iCloud, protected with AES-256 encryption.
• Built natively in Italian, English, Spanish, and French, with more languages planned.

WHY PEOPLE USE IT
Most journaling apps ask you to sit down and write. LifeSync AI does the opposite: it observes the shape of your day in the background and gives you a finished, private summary at night — so keeping a daily record takes seconds, not minutes.
